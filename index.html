Selected WorkKarya Terpilih

From a conversion leak to an offer that pays back.Dari kebocoran konversi ke offer yang balik modal.

Two chapters, one question: where is the money actually being lost? Chapter 1 found the leak by eliminating variables one at a time. Chapter 2 tested the resulting hypothesis on a new product.Dua babak, satu pertanyaan: di mana uangnya sebenarnya bocor? Babak 1 menemukan kebocorannya lewat eliminasi variabel satu per satu. Babak 2 menguji hipotesis hasilnya di produk baru.

CHAPTER 1 / DIAGNOSIS

Diagnosing a conversion leak across four product tests.Mendiagnosis kebocoran konversi di empat pengujian produk.

SetupSetup

Four digital products (Rp69,000–99,000) were each tested with Meta Ads over 3–7 days, totaling Rp2.1M in spend across 7 campaigns. Two of four pixels could not be optimized toward the Purchase event at the outset — one restricted by Meta's signal limits on a finance-adjacent niche, one limited by a new pixel paired with a generic landing page template.Empat produk digital (Rp69.000–99.000) masing-masing diuji dengan Meta Ads selama 3–7 hari, total spend Rp2,1 juta di 7 campaign. Dua dari empat pixel awalnya tidak bisa dioptimasi ke event Purchase — satu terbatas sinyal Meta karena niche berkaitan finance, satu lagi terbatas kombinasi pixel baru dan landing page template generik.

Diagnosis by eliminationDiagnosis lewat eliminasi

Rather than guess at the cause, each candidate variable was tested against the data. Ad CTR stayed healthy above 2.5% throughout, ruling out creative or audience fatigue. Checkout-to-purchase conversion held at 20–33% — above typical e-commerce benchmarks — ruling out a payment problem. That left one point in the funnel unaccounted for: only 3–13% of landing page visitors reached checkout at all.Alih-alih menebak penyebabnya, tiap kandidat variabel diuji lawan data. CTR iklan tetap sehat di atas 2,5% sepanjang periode, menyingkirkan creative/audience fatigue. Checkout-to-purchase bertahan di 20–33% — di atas rata-rata e-commerce — menyingkirkan masalah pembayaran. Tersisa satu titik funnel yang belum terjelaskan: hanya 3–13% pengunjung landing page yang sampai ke checkout.

Thinking / ActionCara Berpikir / Tindakan

On one product, the landing page was rebuilt from the generic template to custom HTML and the pixel reconfigured to track Purchase directly. Purchases rose from 1 to 3 in the following cycle — then plateaued for five days. Fixing the tracking infrastructure opened the bottleneck; it did not, by itself, solve a persuasion problem.Pada salah satu produk, landing page dibangun ulang dari template generik menjadi HTML custom dan pixel dikonfigurasi ulang untuk melacak Purchase langsung. Purchase naik dari 1 ke 3 di siklus berikutnya — lalu mentok 5 hari. Memperbaiki infrastruktur tracking membuka bottleneck-nya; itu saja belum menyelesaikan masalah persuasi.

EvidenceBukti

Rp2.1M spend, 2,528 link clicks, 45 checkouts initiated, 5 purchases across 7 campaigns. Blended CTR 4.18%, blended CPA Rp420,127.Spend Rp2,1jt, 2.528 klik tautan, 45 initiate checkout, 5 purchase di 7 campaign. CTR blended 4,18%, CPA blended Rp420.127.

What it rules outYang tersingkir

Traffic quality and payment friction were both eliminated as causes through direct measurement — not assumption — which is what narrows the problem down to the landing page's persuasion layer.Kualitas traffic dan gesekan pembayaran sama-sama tersingkir sebagai penyebab lewat pengukuran langsung — bukan asumsi — yang mempersempit masalah ke lapisan persuasi landing page.

JudgmentJudgment

The evidence points to the landing page itself — offer clarity, value proposition, price justification — not the ad and not the checkout. This is a working hypothesis from a small sample (3–7 days, 1–3 purchases per product), stated as such rather than as a finished result.Buktinya mengarah ke landing page itu sendiri — kejelasan offer, value proposition, justifikasi harga — bukan iklan dan bukan checkout. Ini hipotesis kerja dari sample kecil (3–7 hari, 1–3 purchase per produk), disampaikan apa adanya, bukan sebagai hasil final.

CHAPTER 2 / TEST

Testing the hypothesis: change the offer, not the ads.Menguji hipotesis: ubah offer-nya, bukan iklannya.

Hypothesis carried overHipotesis yang dibawa

Chapter 1 pointed at the offer layer: what the buyer gets, how clearly it is shown, and whether the price feels justified. So the next product changed the offer itself instead of the targeting or the checkout.Babak 1 menunjuk ke lapisan offer: apa yang didapat pembeli, seberapa jelas ditunjukkan, dan apakah harganya terasa masuk akal. Jadi produk berikutnya mengubah offer-nya, bukan targeting atau checkout.

What changedApa yang diubah

Rasa Pakem: a searchable, offline recipe web app (160 home recipes, measured in spoons and grams) instead of a PDF ebook, priced at Rp49,000, with an optional Rp29,000 printable edition offered at checkout.Rasa Pakem: web app resep yang bisa dicari dan jalan offline (160 resep rumahan, takaran sendok dan gram) menggantikan format ebook PDF, harga Rp49.000, dengan tambahan opsional edisi cetak Rp29.000 di checkout.

EvidenceBukti

Rp3.38M ad spend across 3 campaigns → 74 paid orders, Rp4.67M revenue, ROAS 1.38. Cost per order Rp45,734 against an average order of Rp63,108. 36 of 74 buyers (49%) added the printable edition.Belanja iklan Rp3,38 juta di 3 campaign → 74 order lunas, omzet Rp4,67 juta, ROAS 1,38. Biaya per order Rp45.734 dibanding rata-rata order Rp63.108. 36 dari 74 pembeli (49%) menambah edisi cetak.

Versus Chapter 1Dibanding Babak 1

Cost per order fell from Rp420,127 to Rp45,734. The economics moved from losing money on every sale to a thin but positive return.Biaya per order turun dari Rp420.127 ke Rp45.734. Ekonominya bergeser dari rugi di setiap penjualan ke untung yang tipis tapi positif.

JudgmentJudgment

The hypothesis held: changing the offer moved the result far more than any ad change had. The honest caveat: format, price and the add-on changed at the same time, so I cannot isolate which one did the work, and order counts include buyers who did not come through ads. Isolating those variables is the next test.Hipotesisnya terbukti: mengubah offer menggeser hasil jauh lebih besar daripada perubahan iklan mana pun sebelumnya. Catatan jujurnya: format, harga, dan tambahan berubah bersamaan, jadi saya belum bisa memisahkan mana yang paling berpengaruh, dan jumlah order termasuk pembeli yang tidak datang dari iklan. Memisahkan variabel itu adalah uji berikutnya.

01 / SYSTEMS

From fragmented stock data to a digital control system.Dari data stok yang terfragmentasi menjadi sistem kontrol digital.

ProblemMasalah

Quantity data was available at a general area level, but the relationship between stock quantity, physical location, and the areas that needed control was not sufficiently visible. The problem was therefore not simply data availability. It was a management visibility and control problem.Data quantity tersedia pada gambaran umum area, tetapi hubungan antara jumlah stok, lokasi fisik, dan area yang perlu dikendalikan belum cukup terlihat. Jadi masalahnya bukan sekadar ketersediaan data, melainkan masalah visibilitas dan kontrol dalam sistem manajemen.

DiagnosisDiagnosis

The key gap was the absence of a structured control layer connecting operational data with the physical areas being managed. Without that layer, monitoring remained too dependent on a general view and made it harder to determine where quantity information could be checked and controlled with confidence.Gap utamanya adalah belum adanya lapisan kontrol yang terstruktur untuk menghubungkan data operasional dengan area fisik yang dikelola. Tanpa lapisan tersebut, monitoring masih terlalu bergantung pada gambaran umum dan menyulitkan penentuan area mana yang dapat diperiksa serta dikontrol quantity-nya dengan lebih akurat.

Thinking / ActionCara Berpikir / Tindakan

I designed the Returnable Rack Warehouse System (RRWS) as a management and digitalization concept, not merely an area-mapping exercise. I translated the operational control logic into a structured relationship between area and quantity, then developed a Figma prototype to make that logic visible. I tested the prototype through feedback and iteration so mapped areas could function as a clearer digital control layer for monitoring and managing quantity.Saya merancang Returnable Rack Warehouse System (RRWS) sebagai konsep sistem manajemen dan digitalisasi, bukan sekadar aktivitas pemetaan area. Saya menerjemahkan logika kontrol operasional menjadi hubungan yang lebih terstruktur antara area dan quantity, kemudian mengembangkannya menjadi prototipe Figma agar logika tersebut dapat divisualisasikan. Saya menguji prototipe melalui feedback dan iterasi agar area yang telah termapping dapat berfungsi sebagai lapisan kontrol digital yang lebih jelas untuk memonitor dan mengelola quantity.

EvidenceBukti

The prototype establishes a clearer digital control layer between general stock information and the physical areas being monitored. Mapping is the mechanism; the higher-level outcome is a more structured management view for quantity monitoring and control.Prototipe membentuk lapisan kontrol digital yang lebih jelas antara informasi stok secara umum dengan area fisik yang sedang dimonitor. Mapping adalah mekanismenya; outcome pada level yang lebih tinggi adalah terbentuknya view manajemen yang lebih terstruktur untuk monitoring dan kontrol quantity.

Measured claimKlaim terukur

No percentage is presented because the available evidence does not establish a defensible denominator for a quantified improvement. The defensible claim is about improved visibility and control within the areas that are mapped and brought into the control structure.Tidak ada persentase yang ditampilkan karena evidence yang tersedia belum menetapkan denominator yang cukup kuat untuk menghitung improvement secara kuantitatif. Klaim yang dapat dipertanggungjawabkan adalah peningkatan visibilitas dan kontrol pada area yang sudah termapping dan masuk ke dalam struktur kontrol.

JudgmentJudgment

The important shift was from seeing data to managing information through a control structure. RRWS demonstrates the ability to identify an operational management gap, structure the control logic, translate it into a digital prototype, and use feedback to improve the system. The value is not the map itself. The value is the management layer the map enables.Perubahan pentingnya adalah dari sekadar melihat data menjadi mengelola informasi melalui struktur kontrol. RRWS menunjukkan kemampuan mengidentifikasi gap manajemen operasional, menyusun logika kontrol, menerjemahkannya menjadi prototipe digital, lalu menggunakan feedback untuk memperbaiki sistem. Nilainya bukan pada peta itu sendiri. Nilainya ada pada lapisan manajemen yang dimungkinkan oleh pemetaan tersebut.

Root-cause thinking, in practiceCara berpikir akar masalah, dalam praktik

When a mix-up in material handling caused costly rework, the response wasn't more supervision — it was isolating the variables through root-cause analysis: packaging code and color similarity, then operator fatigue on long shifts. The fix removed the possibility of error at the source (redesigned staging, high-contrast labels, a barcode interlock that physically rejects the wrong part) rather than adding another layer of checking. The same instinct — isolate the variable, fix the root, not the symptom — is what traced the digital conversion leak above from CTR, to checkout rate, to the landing page itself.Saat kesalahan mix-up material menyebabkan rework yang mahal, responsnya bukan menambah pengawasan — tapi mengisolasi variabel lewat root-cause analysis: kemiripan kode dan warna kemasan, lalu faktor keletihan operator di shift panjang. Perbaikannya menghilangkan kemungkinan salah sejak sumbernya (redesain staging, label kontras tinggi, barcode interlock yang menolak fisik part yang salah) — bukan menambah lapisan pengecekan. Insting yang sama — isolasi variabel, perbaiki akar, bukan gejala — yang menelusuri kebocoran konversi digital di atas dari CTR, ke rasio checkout, sampai ke landing page itu sendiri.

AI EVALUATION / IN PRACTICE

Using AI without trusting it blindly.Memakai AI tanpa percaya buta.

ProblemMasalah

AI can generate product ideas, ad scripts and product content faster than they can be checked. Unchecked, it produces confident output that is wrong, generic, or aimed at a market that will not pay.AI bisa menghasilkan ide produk, naskah iklan, dan isi produk lebih cepat daripada kecepatan mengeceknya. Tanpa dicek, hasilnya meyakinkan tapi salah, generik, atau menyasar pasar yang tidak mau bayar.

Thinking / ActionCara Berpikir / Tindakan

I built my own evaluation steps into an AI-assisted product pipeline (idea → gate → market validation → offer → production). Each gate has explicit kill criteria and weighted scoring, and is allowed to pass zero candidates. Examples: an idea gate that rejects red-ocean niches, and a creative gate that ranks ad scripts against budget and account costs before any are produced.Saya membangun langkah evaluasi sendiri di dalam pipeline produk berbantuan AI (ide → gerbang → validasi pasar → offer → produksi). Tiap gerbang punya kriteria gugur yang eksplisit dan skor berbobot, dan boleh meloloskan nol kandidat. Contohnya: gerbang ide yang menolak niche red ocean, dan gerbang creative yang meranking naskah iklan terhadap budget dan biaya akun sebelum ada yang diproduksi.

EvidenceBukti

Rasa Pakem ships 160 recipes and 1,532 cooking steps as a data file written and checked once. The app runs offline with no AI at runtime, so no measurement is ever invented by a model in front of a buyer.Rasa Pakem berisi 160 resep dan 1.532 langkah memasak sebagai file data yang ditulis dan dicek sekali. App-nya jalan offline tanpa AI saat dipakai, jadi tidak ada takaran yang dikarang model di depan pembeli.

Why it mattersMengapa penting

The evaluation question is not "is this output good?" but "what would make me reject it?". Writing that down first is what makes AI output safe to sell.Pertanyaan evaluasinya bukan "apakah output ini bagus?" tapi "apa yang membuat saya menolaknya?". Menuliskan itu lebih dulu yang membuat output AI aman untuk dijual.
